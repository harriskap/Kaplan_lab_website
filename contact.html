Join Us

Work with us

We are building a team of curious, rigorous scientists. We welcome people from diverse scientific backgrounds — molecular biologists, physiologists, physicists, and engineers — who are excited to work on fundamental problems in developmental neuroscience.

Our Culture

What to expect

We aim to be a rigorous, generous, and curious lab. Science is hard, and the best work comes from people who feel supported to take intellectual risks and ask ambitious questions.

The Kaplan Lab is a new lab, which means there is real opportunity to shape the direction of research, develop new approaches, and take ownership of exciting projects from early on. We are committed to doing science that is reproducible, well-controlled, and honestly interpreted. We value open communication, mutual respect, and the idea that good mentorship is a genuine investment — not an afterthought.

We encourage lab members to attend conferences, develop collaborations, and build the skills and independence they need for the next stage of their career. We also recognize that scientists are full people with lives outside the lab, and we support a healthy work-life integration.

Openings

How to apply

01

Graduate Students

We recruit PhD students through the UVA Neuroscience Graduate Program and the Biomedical Sciences Graduate Program. Rotation students are welcome at any time of year — please reach out directly to discuss your interests before applying.

  • E-mail Harris at mvr2qk@virginia.edu with your CV and a brief paragraph on your interests
  • Apply to UVA graduate programs in September–December
  • Rotation projects available year-round
02

Postdoctoral Fellows

We are actively looking for postdocs. Ideal candidates will have experience in at least one of: in vivo physiology, mouse behavior, single-cell genomics, or circuit neuroscience. Enthusiasm and intellectual independence matter more than a precise match to our current techniques.

  • E-mail Harris at mvr2qk@virginia.edu with your CV and a 1-page description of your research interests
  • Include names of 2–3 references
  • Positions available immediately
03

Undergraduates

We welcome motivated undergraduate researchers who can commit to at least one academic year. Research experience in a biology lab, while not required, is helpful. We expect genuine interest in neuroscience and a willingness to learn new skills.

  • E-mail Harris at mvr2qk@virginia.edu with your CV and a brief statement of interest
  • Reach out in the fall for the following spring
  • Credit and work-study options available
